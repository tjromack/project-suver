# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

**This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement")** is entered into as of March 1, 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between **Northwind Analytics LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company (the "Client"), and **Cedar Ridge Consulting, Inc.**, an Illinois corporation (the "Vendor").

---

## 1. Engagement

The Vendor is retained to furnish data-integration and reporting services as described in Exhibit A. The Vendor shall perform the services as an independent contractor and not as an employee, partner, or agent of the Client.

## 2. Term

This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues in force for a period of twenty-four (24) months, unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 8. Upon expiration, the arrangement renews automatically for successive twelve (12) month periods unless either party furnishes written notice of its intent not to renew at least sixty (60) days before the then-current period lapses.

## 3. Compensation

In consideration of the services rendered, the Client shall remit to the Vendor the sum of twelve thousand dollars ($12,000) at the close of each calendar month. Should any monthly disbursement arrive more than fifteen (15) days after its due date, the outstanding balance shall accrue a surcharge equal to one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month.

Amounts owed for partial months shall be prorated on a daily basis using a thirty (30) day month convention.

## 4. Expenses

The Client shall reimburse the Vendor for reasonable out-of-pocket costs incurred in the course of the engagement, provided such costs are supported by contemporaneous receipts and do not, in aggregate, surpass two thousand five hundred dollars ($2,500) in any single month without the Client's prior written authorization.

## 5. Deliverables and Timing

The Vendor shall deliver the initial integrated dataset no later than the forty-fifth (45th) day following the Effective Date. Subsequent reporting packages shall be furnished on a fortnightly cadence thereafter.

## 6. Confidentiality

Each party shall safeguard the other's non-public information and shall refrain from disclosing it to any third party for a period extending three (3) years beyond the conclusion of this Agreement. The obligations of this Section survive termination.

## 7. Intellectual Property

All work product conceived or reduced to practice by the Vendor specifically for the Client under this Agreement shall vest in and become the exclusive property of the Client upon full payment. Pre-existing tools, libraries, and methodologies of the Vendor remain the Vendor's property, and the Vendor grants the Client a perpetual, non-exclusive license to use such materials solely as embedded in the deliverables.

## 8. Termination

Either party may bring this Agreement to an end for convenience by tendering ninety (90) days' advance written notice. Either party may terminate immediately in the event the other commits a material breach that remains uncured thirty (30) days after written notice describing the breach.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

In no event shall either party's cumulative liability arising out of this Agreement exceed the total of the fees paid by the Client during the six (6) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

## 10. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be construed and enforced in accordance with the laws of the State of Illinois, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.
